If something happens

When the siren sounds

  1. Step 1

    Go indoors

    Come inside and stay inside. Close windows and doors.

  2. Step 2

    Get information

    Listen to the news. A battery or wind-up FM radio works when the internet does not.

    DRTV 2

  3. Step 3

    Follow the advice

    Do what the authorities say. Check a source before you share news, so rumours do not spread.

Good to know

Three quick facts

  • Iodine tablets

    Under 40

    Also for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Only for a nuclear accident. Stay indoors and follow the authorities.

    Age bands 0 to 40 highlighted

  • Cooking without power

    Outside only

    A camping stove or barbecue can boil water. Never use it indoors or in a closed space. Smoke and carbon monoxide are dangerous.

  • Call 112

    Real emergencies

    Keep the line free for life-threatening calls. If 112 is down, the authorities will announce other numbers.
