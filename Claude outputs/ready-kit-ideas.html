1Companies

Sponsor a kit for someone who cannot afford one

This builds on the "Got gear for people in need?" section that is already on the site. Companies get a good story to tell, and people in need get a real kit.

  1. STEP 1

    A company pays for kits

    For example 50 kits for older people in its area.

  2. STEP 2

    A local charity hands them out

    The charity knows who needs one. You do not handle names.

  3. STEP 3

    A person in need gets a kit

    Older people living alone, or low-income families.

  4. STEP 4

    The company is thanked on the site

    Name on a small, clearly labeled supporters list.

Workplace pack

3Design

From alarm to hygge

People act when they feel calm and able, not scared. The page can feel like a cozy evening with candles, not like a siren.

Now Alarm

Strong flag red, cool grey, sharp contrast. Clear, but it feels like a warning.

Next Hygge

Softer rosehip red, candle yellow, fjord blue, moss green, a warm pale background. Same readable contrast.
