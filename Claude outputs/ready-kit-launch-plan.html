After step 2

The QR sticker

People see it at home, at work and on notice boards, where the topic is easy to forget. Scan it and the site opens.

Klar til 3 dage?
Scan her 3dageklar.dk Example pattern, not a real code
5–6 cmRound sticker
2 cm or moreSize of the code
Dark on lightWith a white border around the code
Domain as textFor people who do not scan

Do

  • Point the code to 3dageklar.dk/qr. You can change where it goes later without reprinting.
  • Make the code offline, on your own computer.
  • Print on vinyl, so it lasts outside too.

Do not

  • Point it to a temporary vercel.app address.
  • Use "dynamic" QR services. They track scans, or stop working when a trial ends.
  • Put stickers in shared spaces without asking first.
